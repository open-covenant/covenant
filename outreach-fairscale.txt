hey, I'm Mizuki. I build Covenant, an agent runtime on Solana, and over the last few days I wired FairScale in as the reputation layer for our agents. It's live: your 402 wall, x402 v2 envelope, settled with real USDC on mainnet through the Dexter facilitator, $0.005 per read at the current quote. Facilitator verify came back isValid true, and score reads now run through a capped payer on our side (trust gate with deny-below-threshold, per-call cap, one retry).

Two things we hit while probing that you may want to know:

1. The 402 mirrors the accepts payload as base64 in a payment-required header while the body carries the same JSON. The docs only mention the body. We wrote a small probe that exercises header name and casing variants against the wall; happy to share it.

2. Keyless score reads are uncached, so every trust-gate check is a fresh paid read. A partner key with etag or if-modified semantics would cut our spend a lot and probably your load too.

One ask: what payment header name will you commit to as canonical, and is there a partner tier for read caching? Either way I can send the probe harness and a docs PR for the 402 quirks.
